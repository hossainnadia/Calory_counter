Science Behind BMR

Understanding Your Daily Energy Needs

CaloriePulse uses the scientifically validated Harris-Benedict BMR equation to calculate the exact calories your body burns at rest.

Men's BMR Formula

Harris-Benedict Equation

BMR = 66.47 + (13.75 × weight_kg) + (5.003 × height_cm) - (6.755 × age)

Women's BMR Formula

Harris-Benedict Equation

BMR = 655.1 + (9.563 × weight_kg) + (1.850 × height_cm) - (4.676 × age)

How We Calculate Your Daily Targets

Maintenance

Matches your baseline BMR to keep your current weight completely stable.

Weight Loss

Subtracts 500 kcal per day from your BMR to safely reduce ~0.5 kg body weight per week.

Weight Gain

Adds 500 kcal per day to your BMR to support lean muscle and weight gain goals.
